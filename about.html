About

Cybersecurity and digital forensics portfolio

This page explains the professional focus behind the portfolio and the type of knowledge represented here.

Professional Focus

I am building a professional foundation in cybersecurity, digital forensics, malware analysis, reverse engineering, and incident response. This portfolio documents my growth through academic coursework, hands-on labs, CTF participation, technical projects, and structured investigation writeups.

The purpose of this website is to connect my resume to visible evidence of practical skill development. Instead of only listing tools or concepts, this portfolio explains how I apply them, what I learned, and how each experience connects to cybersecurity and digital forensics work.

Academic Background Represented

The content on this site is shaped by cybersecurity knowledge gained through bachelor’s-level study and digital forensics knowledge developed through master’s-level coursework. The focus is not only on theory, but on applying concepts to investigations, analysis workflows, and technical reporting.

Core Areas of Interest

  • Digital forensics investigation workflows
  • Malware analysis and reverse engineering concepts
  • Network traffic analysis and IOC extraction
  • Windows and Linux log review
  • SOC investigation methodology
  • Incident response documentation
  • Evidence handling and forensic reasoning
  • Technical writing and investigation summaries

Documentation Philosophy

Cybersecurity work depends heavily on clear documentation. For that reason, each writeup on this site is designed to explain the objective, process, evidence reviewed, tools used, findings, lessons learned, and professional relevance.

The goal is to present work in a way that is useful to employers, instructors, and technical reviewers without exposing private data, protected lab answers, challenge flags, or sensitive artifacts.
